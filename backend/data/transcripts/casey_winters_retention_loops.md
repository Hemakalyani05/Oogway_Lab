# Episode: Casey Winters on Why Most Companies Fail at Retention & Growth Loops
**Guest:** Casey Winters (Former Chief Product Officer at Eventbrite, Growth Lead at Pinterest & Grubhub)
**Host:** Lenny Rachitsky
**Date:** 2023-08-22
**URL:** https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/casey-winters-retention-loops

---

[00:01:20] Lenny Rachitsky: Casey, you've helped scale Pinterest, Grubhub, and Eventbrite. When startups struggle to grow, you almost always diagnose it as a retention problem rather than an acquisition problem. Why?

[00:03:00] Casey Winters: Because acquisition is just pouring water into a bucket. If the bottom of the bucket is missing—which is retention—no amount of paid ads, influencer marketing, or PR will save the company. True sustainable growth comes when your user retention curve flattens parallel to the x-axis over time.

[00:06:15] Casey Winters: There are two critical retention concepts every growth leader must master:
1. **The Shape of the Retention Curve:** If your cohort curve trends towards zero over 6 to 12 months, you do not have Product-Market Fit. If it stabilizes at 20%, 40%, or 60% and remains flat, you have a solid retaining core user base that you can build growth loops on top of.
2. **Loops vs. Funnels:** Traditional marketing models look at funnels (Top of Funnel -> Mid Funnel -> Bottom of Funnel). But funnels are linear—you have to constantly buy more traffic. Loops are closed systems where one cohort's actions naturally generate the next cohort of users.

[00:09:40] Lenny Rachitsky: What are the main types of growth loops?

[00:11:10] Casey Winters:
1. **Viral/Referral Loops:** A user invites friends to participate (e.g., Slack, WhatsApp, Dropbox space referrals).
2. **Content/SEO Loops:** Users create content (public boards, reviews, questions) that gets indexed by search engines, attracting new organic visitors who become creators (e.g., Pinterest, Quora, Yelp).
3. **Paid Loops:** Revenue from user LTV is reinvested into profitable ad channels with a payback period under 12 months.
