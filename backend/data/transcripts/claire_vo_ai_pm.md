# Episode: Claire Vo on AI Tools, ChatPRD, and the Future of Product Management
**Guest:** Claire Vo (Chief Product Officer at LaunchDarkly, Creator of ChatPRD)
**Host:** Lenny Rachitsky
**Date:** 2024-03-12
**URL:** https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/claire-vo-ai-pm

---

[00:01:10] Lenny Rachitsky: Claire, you are the CPO of LaunchDarkly and you also built ChatPRD in your spare time, which exploded to over 100,000 active PM users. How is AI fundamentally altering the role of a Product Manager?

[00:02:45] Claire Vo: Lenny, we are living through the greatest productivity inflection in tech history. Historically, product managers spent 60% of their week writing boilerplate documents: user stories, acceptance criteria, technical specs, and meeting summaries. With modern LLMs and agentic tools, that 60% drops to 5 minutes.

[00:05:10] Claire Vo: The new superpower for product managers is **Velocity and Taste**. If a PM can draft an initial PRD in 10 minutes, generate clickable prototypes with AI code tools in an hour, and test it with 5 customers by the afternoon, they out-iterate traditional teams by a factor of ten.

[00:08:20] Lenny Rachitsky: What makes a good AI-assisted PRD?

[00:09:40] Claire Vo: A good AI PRD is not just raw ChatGPT output. You have to feed the AI specific user personas, business objectives, edge cases, and constraints. The structure should include:
1. **Problem Statement & Customer Evidence:** Why does this matter?
2. **Key Success Metrics:** How do we measure success?
3. **Non-Goals:** What are we explicitly NOT building in v1?
4. **User Flows & Edge Cases:** What happens when things fail?
5. **Technical Feasibility & Open Questions.**

[00:13:00] Claire Vo: The PMs who will thrive in the next decade are the ones who embrace AI as a thought partner—using it to brainstorm edge cases, red-team assumptions, and rapidly prototype interactive UI artifacts.
