# Episode: Elena Verna on B2B Product-Led Growth (PLG), Product-Led Sales, and Growth Loops
**Guest:** Elena Verna (Growth Advisor, Head of Growth at Lovable, ex-Miro, Amplitude, SurveyMonkey)
**Host:** Lenny Rachitsky
**Date:** 2023-11-14
**URL:** https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/elena-verna-b2b-growth

---

[00:01:20] Lenny Rachitsky: Elena, welcome back to the podcast. You are widely regarded as one of the top authorities in B2B growth, having helped scale Miro, Amplitude, Dropbox, and SurveyMonkey. Let's start with Product-Led Growth (PLG) in B2B. What is the fundamental difference between PLG and traditional Sales-Led growth?

[00:03:10] Elena Verna: Thanks Lenny. In a traditional Sales-Led model, the product is locked behind a demo and a salesperson. The buyer is an executive with budget authority who buys based on promises. In Product-Led Growth, the product itself drives acquisition, activation, retention, and monetization. The end user discovers the product, experiences the "aha moment" immediately for free, and becomes the internal champion who brings it to their team.

[00:05:40] Elena Verna: PLG is not just freemium; it is an organizational operating system. You are optimizing for time-to-value (TTV). If a user signs up and cannot solve their problem within three minutes, you have lost them.

[00:08:15] Lenny Rachitsky: You often speak about Product-Led Sales (PLS). How does sales fit into a PLG motion?

[00:09:40] Elena Verna: This is where so many companies stumble. They think PLG means you fire your sales team. In reality, PLG creates the highest quality top-of-funnel pipeline for sales. We call this Product Qualified Leads (PQLs) or Product Qualified Accounts (PQAs). Instead of cold outbound calling, sales reps reach out to accounts that already have 20 active users on a free tier sharing boards or documents. Sales doesn't sell "features"; sales sells enterprise governance, security, SSO, and centralized billing.

[00:13:00] Elena Verna: When structuring growth loops, there are two primary B2B engines: Organic Collaboration Loops (like Miro or Figma, where user A invites user B to complete a workflow) and Content/SEO Loops. If you rely exclusively on paid acquisition in B2B, your unit economics will collapse as CAC increases.

[00:16:30] Lenny Rachitsky: What is the number one metric growth teams should focus on first?

[00:17:45] Elena Verna: Focus on User Activation Rate before monetization. If you try to monetize a leaky bucket, you burn brand capital and money. Define your core activation milestone—e.g. creating 1 board with 2 collaborators within 7 days—and relentlessly remove onboarding friction until that rate hits at least 40% to 50%.
