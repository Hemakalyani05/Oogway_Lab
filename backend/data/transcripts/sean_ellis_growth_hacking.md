[00:01:05] Lenny Rachitsky: Sean, you literally coined the term "growth hacker" and wrote the definitive book *Hacking Growth*. How do you define a growth system today?

[00:02:40] Sean Ellis: A growth system is a disciplined, data-driven cadence of cross-functional experimentation. It is not a bag of sneaky marketing tricks; it is the scientific method applied to customer acquisition, activation, and retention.

[00:05:15] Sean Ellis: To run an effective growth engine, you need three core elements:
1. **The North Star Metric:** A single key metric that best captures the core value your product delivers to customers. For Airbnb, it's Nights Booked; for Spotify, it's Time Spent Listening; for Slack, it's Daily Active Users sending 10+ messages.
2. **High-Tempo Testing:** Teams that run 5 to 10 experiments per week will always out-learn and out-perform teams running 1 experiment per month. Even with a 30% win rate, high testing volume compounds into exponential growth.
3. **The ICE Prioritization Framework:** Rate every growth experiment idea from 1 to 10 on three dimensions:
   - **Impact:** How much will this move the North Star Metric if it works?
   - **Confidence:** How certain are we based on prior data or user research?
   - **Ease:** How fast and inexpensive is it to build and test?
   Take the average score (Impact + Confidence + Ease / 3) to rank the backlog.
